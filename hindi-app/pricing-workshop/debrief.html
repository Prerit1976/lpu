AI as your pricing co-pilot · 12 min

How real teams price with AI today

Pricing analysts increasingly use AI to pressure-test decisions in seconds — modelling competitor moves, drafting rationales, and stress-testing “what if we raise 10%?”. AI won’t decide for you, but it makes you a faster, sharper decision-maker. The skill is asking well.

This is a real, ₹-billion industry
Uber / Ola

Algorithms set surge prices second-by-second from live demand.

Airlines

Yield management — the same seat changes price hundreds of times before takeoff.

Amazon

Reprices millions of items a day to beat rivals and read demand.

Swiggy / BookMyShow

Fees and tickets flex with rain, timing and hype.

Behind all of it: data + models making pricing calls faster than any human could. You just played a hand-run version of exactly this.

❌ A weak prompt

What price should I sell my product at?

No context. You’ll get a generic, useless answer.

✅ A strong prompt

Act as a pricing strategist. My product is a ₹40,000 smartphone that costs ₹24,000 to make. Two rivals sell similar phones at ₹38,000 and ₹42,000. My customers are moderately price-sensitive. My goal is maximum profit, not market share. Recommend a price, explain the trade-off in 3 bullets, and tell me one risk.

Role + numbers + rivals + customer + goal = a decision-grade answer.

🎤 Trainer says The formula for any good pricing prompt: Role · your costs · competitor prices · customer type · your goal · ask for the trade-off and the risk. Have teams paste their own simulation numbers and compare the AI’s advice to what they actually did.
