Facilitator Run-of-Show

A minute-by-minute script for the 3-hour workshop. You do not need to be a pricing expert — every screen carries its own notes, and this guide tells you exactly what to say and click. Read it once; keep it open on your phone during class.

Before you start (5 min setup)

  • Open the workshop on the projector laptop: start at the home page. Bookmark all five pages.
  • Test the projector and that your browser fills the screen (press F11 / green button for fullscreen).
  • Form teams of 5–6 students each (aim for 8–10 teams). Give each a team name or let them pick.
  • Hand out one sheet of paper per team to work out their price privately each round.
  • The floating timer (bottom-right of every page) is yours — tap ▶ to start, it turns red near the end.
💡 The one thing to remember Your job is not to teach pricing theory — it’s to run the game and ask good questions. The content teaches itself. Keep energy high and let the leaderboard do the talking.

Minute-by-minute

0:00
10m

Welcome & the hook

Open home page. Introduce the day: “Today you don’t take notes — you run companies.”
🎤 “By the end of today, one team in this room will have out-priced everyone else and won the market. Everyone will leave knowing how Apple, Zomato and your local chai stall actually decide their prices. Let’s go.”

30m

Module 1 · Pricing Fundamentals

Open Module 1. Walk the three strategies. Do the Cost-Plus calculator live — ask a student to shout a markup. Tap through the coffee value-ladder. Run the 3-question quiz as a call-out.
🎤 “Same coffee, ₹60 to ₹600. Nothing changed but the story around it. That gap is where the real money in pricing lives.”
0:40
30m

Module 2 · Customer Economics

Open Module 2. Spend most time on the profit-finder chart — drag the price, find the peak, then switch customer types and watch the peak move. Show break-even: drag price down, watch break-even units explode.
🎤 “Profit is a hill, not a ramp. Too cheap you give it away, too dear everyone leaves. A good CEO hunts for the top of that hill — and you’re about to do it for real.”
1:10
10m

Simulation setup & briefing

Open Simulation console. Pick a scenario (Smartphone or Coffee), set the number of teams and 6 rounds. Read the scenario story aloud. Explain the one rule: each round, set one price.
🎤 “You are now the CEO. Each round you set one number — your price. It decides how many customers you win and how much profit you keep. Your rivals are sitting right here. Ready?”
1:20
70m

Simulation · 6 competitive rounds

Per round (~10 min): (1) show the round screen & read any market shock aloud; (2) give teams 60–90s to decide on paper; (3) collect prices and type each into the console; (4) hit Run the market; (5) reveal results, read the leaderboard, ask “why did they win?”; (6) Next round.
🎤 After each round: “Before I tell you the lesson — you tell me. Why did the top team win this round?” Let them debate 30 seconds, then reveal the shock’s lesson on screen.
⏱ Running late? Do 4–5 rounds instead of 6 — just set fewer rounds at setup, or stop and hit “See final results” early is not available, so plan rounds up front.
2:30
30m

Module 4 · AI Pricing & Debrief

Open Debrief. Crown the winner, ask their strategy. Reveal the 4 lessons by asking teams, not telling. Show the weak-vs-strong AI prompt, run the live Pricing Advisor with a team’s real numbers. End with the 3 reflection questions.
🎤 “The winners didn’t have luck — they had a reason for every price. That reason is the skill you’re taking home. And now you know how to make AI your co-pilot for it.”
2:55
5m

Close & certificates

On the Debrief page, generate a participation certificate (type name → Print/Save PDF). Thank the room. Point them to the home page to replay any module.

If a student asks…

“Why did the expensive team win?”
Because profit = margin × volume. They kept more on each sale, and didn’t lose enough buyers to matter. Cheap isn’t automatically smart.

“What’s the ‘correct’ price?”
There isn’t one — it depends on your cost, your rivals, and your customers. That’s the whole point. The best price is a decision, not a formula.

“Isn’t undercutting always good?”
No — a price war shrinks everyone’s margin. Sometimes holding firm or going premium wins. The leaderboard proves it.

“Is the simulation using real data?”
It’s a realistic teaching model, not live market data — built so the trade-offs feel true, which is what matters for learning.

“The numbers look off / I mistyped a price.”
The game auto-saves. If needed, hit “New game” (top-right) to restart cleanly — you’ll only lose the current game’s scores.

Tech & troubleshooting

  • No internet needed. Everything runs in the browser. Copy the pricing-workshop folder to the laptop and open index.html, or use the hosted link.
  • Auto-save: the simulation survives an accidental refresh — your rounds are kept.
  • Projector too small? Zoom the browser with Ctrl/Cmd +.
  • Print certificates / this guide: use the browser’s Print → “Save as PDF”.
  • Works on any modern browser (Chrome, Edge, Safari, Firefox). No install, no login.
