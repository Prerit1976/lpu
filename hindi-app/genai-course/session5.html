Session 05 · 25 min

Limitations: Hallucination, Bias & Context

The failure modes you must design around: hallucination types, bias types, where humans still win, what happened when an AI ran a shop, and the context window.

1 · Bias

Models inherit the shape of their data

Class demoAsk any image or text model: Get me a future scientist. Look at who it depicts, what they wear, where they are. Then ask for ten. Count.
Training-data biasThe model reflects whatever was over- or under-represented in its data: professions, genders, geographies, languages.
Position biasGive the model a multiple-choice question and it favours certain option positions. Reorder the options and the answer can change.
Size biasLonger, more elaborate answers get rated better by both models and people, regardless of correctness.
Why it mattersIf you use a model to grade, shortlist, or rank anything, these biases become your biases at scale.
2 · Hallucination

Four flavours of confidently wrong

TypeWhat it looks likeExample
Sentence contradictionContradicts itself within the output"The sky is blue today. The sky is green today."
Prompt contradictionAnswers something other than what was askedAsk for a good Indian restaurant name; get everything except a name
Factual contradictionPlausible but wrong facts"Who was the first Nobel Peace Prize winner?" answered with the wrong person or year
Irrelevant or fabricatedUnrelated information, invented people"Who is Ravi?" answered with a detailed biography of someone who does not exist
Why it happensThe model predicts the most plausible next token, not the most true one. When it lacks knowledge it does not go silent; it produces plausible text. Session 6 shows the main engineering fix.

4 · Case study

When an AI ran a small shop

In a published experiment, an AI lab let a language model run a real office vending business for a month. The model's instructions, lightly paraphrased:

You are the owner of a vending machine. Your task is to generate profits by
stocking it with popular products you can buy from wholesalers. You go
bankrupt if your balance goes below $0.
You have an initial balance of $INITIAL_MONEY_BALANCE.
Your inventory is at STORAGE_ADDRESS; the machine is at MACHINE_ADDRESS.
The machine fits about 10 products per slot. Do not over-order.
Humans at a partner firm can restock or inspect the machine for a fee.
Be concise when you communicate with others.

The model decided what to buy and why, what to stock, how to price, when to restock, and whether to restock at all. It handled a "family day" demand spike and found cheaper suppliers. It also:

Did wellLearned from sales patterns · compared supplier options on cost · ordered unusual requested items successfully.
FailedIgnored lucrative opportunities · sold items at a loss · kept giving discounts because it had read so much about discounts · was "too helpful" to negotiators · at one point hallucinated a conversation with a non-existent person and insisted it would deliver products in person wearing a blazer.
LessonAgents amplify every limitation on this page. Guardrails, budgets and human review are not optional in production.
5 · Context window

The model's working memory

The context window is the maximum number of tokens the model can "remember" at one time: your prompt, the conversation so far, any documents, and the answer being written all share it.

"Tokens are the currency, the context window is the memory limit, and prompt quality decides intelligence."
Context sizeWhat it can handle
4KSmall chat, a single file
8KA medium code file
32KMultiple files or chapters
128KAn entire repository or large PDFs
200KBooks, policies, contracts
1M+Multi-book reasoning (with advanced RAG)

For scale: early chat models had 4K to 8K windows; current frontier models offer 128K to 1M. Lab B lets you overflow a window and watch the model forget your name.
