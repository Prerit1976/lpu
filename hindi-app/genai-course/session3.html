Session 03 · 20 min

The LLM Landscape

Open vs closed models, API vs self-hosted vs edge deployment, and an engineering rule of thumb for choosing between them.

1 · Big picture

Four axes to understand any model choice

Model ownershipOpen vs closed weights.
Control vs convenienceSelf-hosted vs API.
EconomicsToken cost, infrastructure cost, scaling cost.
Risk and complianceData privacy, IP leakage, regulatory exposure.
2 · Open models

Weights you can download

Models whose weights are available, fully or partially, so teams can run, fine-tune and customise them.

FamilyKnown for
Llama (Meta)Strong general reasoning, widely adopted in enterprises
MistralHigh performance at smaller sizes, excellent cost-efficiency
Gemma (Google)Lightweight, optimised for research and controlled use
Qwen (Alibaba)Strong multilingual and coding capability
Why engineers like themFull control over data and infra · fine-tuning for domain workflows · no per-token vendor lock-in · better fit for regulated environments.
Key limitationsYou manage GPUs, scaling, latency and monitoring · requires MLOps maturity · performance may lag frontier closed models.

Summary: open models are ideal when control, customisation or compliance matters more than raw intelligence.

3 · Closed models

Accessed only through a vendor API

You never see the weights, only inputs and outputs.

FamilyKnown for
GPT (OpenAI)Strong general reasoning, mature ecosystem
Claude (Anthropic)Long context, strong on safety and alignment
Gemini (Google)Multimodal, deep Google ecosystem integration
StrengthsState-of-the-art reasoning · zero infra management · fast product integration · continuous upgrades.
RisksData residency and privacy · vendor lock-in · cost unpredictability at scale · limited customisation.

Summary: closed models optimise for speed, quality and developer velocity: ideal for rapid innovation and prototypes.

4 · Trade-offs

Cost, performance and privacy side by side

DimensionOpen-source LLMClosed LLM
PerformanceMedium to high (tunable)Very high
Cost modelInfra + opsPer-token pricing
Data privacyFull controlVendor governed
Fine-tuningFullLimited or none
Time to marketSlowerVery fast
Lock-in riskLowHigh
Engineering rule of thumbPrototype → closed model. Production + compliance → open model. Scale → hybrid: route easy traffic to a cheap open model and hard cases to a frontier API.
5 · Deployment

API, self-hosted or edge

FactorAPI-basedSelf-hostedEdge (on device)
ControlLowVery highFull
Setup timeMinutesWeeksWeeks to months
Cost modelUsage-basedInfra-basedOne-time + tuning
PrivacyVendor-dependentFull controlMaximum
LatencyMediumLowVery low
ScalabilityAutomaticManual or autoDevice-bound
Decision exercise · 10 minFor each scenario pick open/closed and API/self-hosted/edge, and justify in one line: (a) a hospital summarising patient notes, (b) a startup demoing a sales assistant next week, (c) a factory sensor flagging defects without internet, (d) a bank's customer chatbot at 10 million conversations a month.
