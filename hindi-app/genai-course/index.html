About this course

GenAI Foundations is the public, self-paced version of a programme Poorit Technologies delivers to engineering faculty, students and corporate teams. The emphasis is intuition first, jargon second, and everything is demonstrated rather than merely described.

Who it is for: faculty, engineers, product and business teams who use AI tools daily and want to understand what is happening underneath.

Poorit Technologies builds engineering and business capability for campuses and companies: practitioner-led training, Centres of Excellence and assessment through IntelliTest Hub.
