Quick reference: the seven teaching categories

1. Artificial Intelligence (AI)The broad field of systems performing tasks associated with intelligence.
2. Machine Learning (ML)Learns patterns from data for prediction, classification or recommendations.
3. Deep LearningUses multi-layer neural networks to learn complex patterns.
4. Generative AICreates new content such as text, images, audio, video or code.
5. Large Language Model (LLM)A model trained to process and generate language.
6. ChatGPTA specific AI product/interface that may use LLMs and generative AI.
7. AI AgentA system designed to pursue goals through multiple steps, tools and actions under permissions.

Important: These are not seven strictly nested technical layers. ChatGPT is a product, while AI agents are a system-design approach. A real application can fit several categories.
